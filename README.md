## ✨ Features

* 💖 **Pastel Aesthetic Design:** Soft lavender gradients, elegant royal crown typography, and smooth visual feedback.
* 🍓 **Addictive Match-3 Gameplay:** Swap and match sweet fruits to score points and build high-score streaks.
* 🚀 **Adventure Mode:** Click **Start Adventure** to plunge straight into the gameplay grid.
* 🔄 **Progress Tracking:** Easily save or reset your score and progress whenever you choose.
* 📱 **Fully Responsive:** Play seamlessly on desktop monitors, tablets, and mobile web browsers.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Visit the live game link: [https://tethi04.github.io/Princess-s-Fruit-Crunch/](https://tethi04.github.io/Princess-s-Fruit-Crunch/)
2. Click **Start Adventure** on the main menu to begin.
3. Swap adjacent fruit tiles to match **3 or more** identical fruits in a row or column.
4. Clear as many matches as possible to maximize your score!
5. Use **Reset Progress** if you ever want to restart your adventure from scratch.
